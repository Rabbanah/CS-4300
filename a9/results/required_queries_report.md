### Final Output

Under FDA Food Code 2026 § 2-201.12(A)(1), the person in charge must exclude a food employee from the food establishment if the employee is symptomatic with vomiting or diarrhea, except when the symptom is from a noninfectious condition [fda-food-code-2026.pdf::p0059::c00]. Therefore, if the employee has vomiting or diarrhea and there is no known noninfectious condition, the person in charge must exclude the employee. Under § 2-201.13(A)(1), the employee may be reinstated if the employee is asymptomatic for at least 24 hours, or if the employee provides the person in charge with written medical documentation from a health practitioner stating that the symptom is from a noninfectious condition [fda-food-code-2026.pdf::p0061::c00].

If the employee is diagnosed with certain pathogens such as Norovirus, Shigella spp., STEC, hepatitis A virus, or typhoid fever, additional exclusion/restriction and regulatory-authority approval requirements may apply [fda-employee-health-personal-hygiene-handbook.pdf::p0031::c00].
