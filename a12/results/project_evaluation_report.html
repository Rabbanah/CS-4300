Motivating U0-HW-11 Baseline Evidence: In U0-HW-11 (b9ad421), the baseline agent (smc_baseline) suffered peak single-step latency of 49.41 ms on Case 01 (and 73.81 ms with 45 steps exceeding 50 ms across 30 seeds) due to unconditional 3-sweep full-domain MCMC scans, and lost by 2 shots to hunt_target on the corner-clustered adversarial board (56 vs. 54). To evaluate whether our planned changes helped, smc_baseline is preserved verbatim alongside two controlled ablations in a12/belief_agent.py:

  • Refinement A — Cell-Indexed Placement Tables & ESS-Gated Adaptive MCMC (smc_refined): Pre-indexes valid ship placements by occupied cell bit so hit-constrained Gibbs and two-ship block moves inspect only O(k) (≤ 8) covering placements instead of scanning all 120–180 domain placements, uses rejection-first unconstrained sampling, and gates MCMC sweep depth by survival/diversity ratio (1 lightweight sweep without two-ship coupling when survived ≥ 0.65N on routine misses; 2–3 sweeps under heavier pruning). Expected effect: 3–5× latency reduction and elimination of >50 ms spikes while preserving 100% particle constraint consistency.
  • Refinement B — Exact One-Step Expected Percept Information Gain (smc_info_gain): Builds on Refinement A and computes exact multi-outcome mutual information I(F; Yc | history) = H(Yc) over MISS, HIT, and ship-specific SUNK(s) percepts in a single pass over active particles, paired with certainty exploitation (P(occc) = 1.0) and composite score P(occc) + 0.35 · I(F; Yc). Expected effect: Improved discrimination on touching/ambiguous ships and higher information gain per probe within the 50 ms budget.

5. Variation, Limitations, and Scope of Interpretation

  • Sample-Size Boundary (30 Paired Seeds): Across 30 standard 10×10 seeds, shot counts varied substantially (smc_refined range 31–55, std=6.29; smc_info_gain range 26–62, std=8.39). While 30 seeds cleanly separate Bayesian SMC from hunt_target (non-overlapping 95% CIs, 29/30 paired wins), 30 seeds cannot prove the U0-HW-10 ≥ 99.5% reliability criterion or resolve small (~1.5-shot) differences between smc_refined and smc_baseline.
  • Non-Uniform Adversarial Layout Vulnerability: Both eval_04 (corner cluster: 65 shots) and eval_05 (dense touching cluster: 59 shots for smc_refined vs. 32 for hunt_target's orthogonal flood-fill) show that uniform-prior Bayesian inference loses its advantage when ships are intentionally placed against the prior distribution.
  • Why One-Step Percept IG Underperformed max_prob on Average: On a 10×10 board where early-hunt cell probabilities are < 0.5, binary percept entropy H(p) is monotonically increasing in p (preserving the same center bias as max_prob), while in target mode weighting I(F; Yc) occasionally delayed high-probability (P ≈ 0.80) finish shots to probe P ≈ 0.50 splits, raising variance (std=8.39 vs. 6.29).
  • Environment Assumptions: Results apply strictly to single-agent, static, noise-free, known-manifest 10×10 Battleship. Noisy sensors violate the exact likelihood model and intentionally trigger BELIEF_COLLAPSE (eval_10).
