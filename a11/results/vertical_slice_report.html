2. Implemented Agent-Environment Path and Changes from U0-HW-10

The vertical slice converts the 6×6 exhaustive Proof of Concept (a10/poc_battleship.py) into a full 10×10 five-ship system in a11/ with strict separation between the hidden-state simulator (battleship_env.py), the Sequential Bayesian Belief-Tracking agent (belief_agent.py), and the application-controlled execution & telemetry loop (runner.py).

Alignment and Material Refinements from U0-HW-10

Design Component U0-HW-10 Specification Implemented Vertical Slice & Justification
Exact Likelihood Conditioning Filter hypotheses by hits, misses, and sunk ship identities. Implemented via 100-bit integer bitmasks conditioning on both sunk_ship and the exact sinking coordinate + cumulative hits mask at the sink step. Guarantees exact Bayesian equivalence to full percept history while cutting per-check cost to O(1) bitwise ops.
Particle Deprivation Recovery SMC particle set with local MCMC ship-perturbation rejuvenation (flagged as principal uncertainty). Added randomized MRV hit-covering backtracking repair when survived_particles == 0, paired with exact single-ship Gibbs sweeps (100% acceptance, preserves uniform posterior) and coupled two-ship block moves to break adjacent-ship lock-in. Exhaustive tree pruning also proves unsatisfiability on corrupted observations.
Exact Endgame Fallback Switch to exact enumeration when remaining hypotheses drop below a threshold. Triggered automatically when remaining per-ship domain product is tractable (≤ 60,000, or ≤ 120,000 with ≤ 2 unsunk ships) and total consistent fleets ≤ 4,000. Reproduces the exact 2,472 hypotheses on the 6×6 PoC and activates seamlessly mid-game on 10×10 (Step 28 in Case 01).
Failure & Trace Boundaries Victory within B_max; timeout or invalid action failure. Added explicit EpisodeStatus enum (VICTORY, TIMEOUT, BELIEF_COLLAPSE, INVALID_CONFIG, INVALID_ACTION) and per-step JSON trace recording (available actions, top-5 candidates, particle survival, board entropy reduction, latency).

4. Current Omissions and Known Failures

  • Deferred Expected Information Gain Policy (smc_info_gain): Only the arg max P(occ_r,c) policy is implemented in this vertical slice; one-step expected Shannon entropy reduction is scheduled for the next checkpoint (per U0-HW-10 Week 3).
  • Deferred Multi-Seed Statistical Evaluation: The 1,000-episode paired benchmark across B_max ∈ {60, 75, 100} with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals and the optional no-touching rule variant are deferred to the November 19 evaluation checkpoint.
  • Known Weakness — Corner/Edge Bias on Adversarial Layouts: On case_02_corner_clustered_boundary, smc_max_prob took 14 consecutive misses (11 in the interior 8×8 region) before its first hit and trailed hunt_target by 2 shots (56 vs. 54).
  • Known Weakness — Latency Spikes Under Heavy Pruning: Unconditional 3-sweep MCMC rejuvenation on every step where survived < 250 produced a peak step latency of 49.41 ms on case_01, leaving minimal margin below the 50 ms U0-HW-10 target.
  • Explicitly Handled Failure Boundaries: The agent assumes a known fleet manifest and noise-free deterministic percepts; budget exhaustion (TIMEOUT), invalid grid/fleet specs (INVALID_CONFIG), invalid probes (INVALID_ACTION), and contradictory percepts (BELIEF_COLLAPSE) terminate explicitly with diagnostic records rather than silent recovery.

5. Evidence-Based Change Planned for Next Checkpoint (Nov 19)

Planned Change: Implement Expected Information Gain (smc_info_gain) top-K one-step entropy lookahead paired with ESS-gated MCMC rejuvenation and cell-indexed placement tables.

Empirical Support from Baseline Results:

  1. Addressing Corner-Clustered Degradation (Case 02): smc_max_prob lost 2 shots to hunt_target (56 vs. 54) and achieved lower entropy reduction (1.154 vs. 1.243 bits/shot) due to interior center bias. Evaluating expected posterior entropy reduction across the top-K candidate cells rewards probes that maximally partition both interior and perimeter fleet hypotheses.
  2. Creating Latency Headroom for Lookahead (Case 01): Peak single-step latency reached 49.41 ms during unconditional rejuvenation (27 events in Case 01, 33 in Case 02). Triggering MCMC rejuvenation only when effective survival drops below 0.6N (or unique diversity falls below 0.7N) and pre-indexing ship placements by cell bit will reduce peak SMC update latency below ~20 ms, ensuring smc_info_gain stays safely under the 50 ms/shot deployment criterion across the 1,000-episode evaluation.
